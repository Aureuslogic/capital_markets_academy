The Founder's Story

The market taught Sam the lesson. He built the system so you learn it faster.

Every serious trader eventually meets the same opponent. It is not the central banks, the algorithms or the spread. It is the person holding the mouse.

Sam Onigbanjo learned this the way most traders do: live, in the forex and commodities markets, trading currencies, gold, oil and the Nasdaq. The charts could be read. The news could be studied. Yet he watched capable traders around him, people fluent in technical analysis, give their gains back through revenge trades, oversized positions and the quiet decision to move a stop loss "just this once".

His conclusion was simple and it changed everything: the industry had an education problem, not an information problem. There were thousands of courses teaching entries and exits. Almost none taught the trader how to govern their own behaviour when real money moved against them.

So he built the answer himself. Drawing on his trading experience, his training at Saïd Business School, University of Oxford, and a career delivering serious projects, including leadership on a £77 million local council initiative and a global portal built for the Nigerian government, Sam founded Aulteus Logic™ and created the EIT™ (Emotionally Intelligent Trader) framework: six protocols that combine market intelligence, emotional intelligence and execution discipline into one system a trader runs every single day.

Capital Markets Academy is that framework made teachable. Over 5,000 traders across the UK have now trained here, live, on FCA-regulated platforms, learning to trade technically, to read the news and geopolitics, and to master the one variable no indicator can measure: themselves.

"I am a builder first and a teacher second. I do not teach theories I read somewhere. I teach systems I built, tested and trade with myself."

Sam Onigbanjo, Founder, Capital Markets Academy
