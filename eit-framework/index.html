What is the EIT™ trading framework?

The system at the centre of Capital Markets Academy. Built to govern the one part of trading no indicator can measure: the trader.

Created by Sam Onigbanjo, founder of Capital Markets Academy, under Aulteus Logic™
DefinitionThe Aulteus Logic™ EIT™ (Emotionally Intelligent Trader) framework is a proprietary trading discipline system made of six protocols. It combines market intelligence, emotional intelligence and execution discipline into one routine a trader runs every trading day, and it judges a trader by whether they traded intelligently, not only by whether they made money.

Why it exists

Most trading education teaches entries and exits. Very little teaches a trader how to behave when real money moves against them. Yet revenge trading, overtrading, oversized positions and moved stop losses are behind a great many damaged accounts, and none of them is an analysis problem. The EIT™ framework was built to close that gap: a behavioural system that sits alongside your strategy and governs when you may trade, and when you must stop.

The three things it trains

  • Market intelligence. Technical skill, plus the ability to read news and geopolitics and turn them into informed decisions.
  • Emotional intelligence. Recognising your own state before it makes decisions for you.
  • Execution discipline. Rules that hold under pressure, reinforced by live coaching and accountability groups.

Protocol 01: the Traffic Light System

The first protocol is the only one we publish by name. Before any trade, the trader rates their own emotional state as green, amber or red, and that rating decides whether they may trade at all, and how. It checks the person, not the chart. Read the full guide to the Traffic Light Check.

Protocols 02 to 06

The remaining five protocols are taught inside the Academy. Together they build on the Traffic Light System to cover how a trader prepares, executes, reviews and stays accountable. We keep them for members because they work best taught live, with feedback, rather than read once and forgotten.

We don’t only ask “did you make money?” We ask “did you trade intelligently?”

Who it is for

Beginners who want good habits from the first trade, experienced traders whose results do not reflect their skill, and professionals who want structure that fits around a full diary. Every Capital Markets Academy programme is built on it.
