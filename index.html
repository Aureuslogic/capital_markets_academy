Frequently Asked Questions

Serious questions from serious traders.

What is the best way to learn forex trading in London or the UK?

Live education with professional traders, not pre-recorded videos. We run live daily coaching sessions, weekly live trading rooms and Sunday market overviews, training students in technical analysis, news and geopolitics, and trading psychology on FCA-regulated platforms. You learn in real market conditions, with real feedback, from traders who trade every day.

How do I stop revenge trading and overtrading?

Revenge trading and overtrading are emotional habits, not analytical ones, so they cannot be fixed with another indicator. They require a behavioural system. Our EIT™ framework uses six protocols, including the pre-trade Traffic Light check and structured accountability groups, to govern when you may trade and when you must stop. Members retrain the habit at its source.

What is the EIT (Emotionally Intelligent Trader) framework?

EIT™ is our proprietary trading discipline framework, built on six protocols that combine market intelligence, emotional intelligence and execution discipline. The first protocol, the Traffic Light System, is public. The remaining five are taught inside the Academy.

Is forex trading education safe and regulated?

Education itself is not regulated, but the platforms you trade on are. We train students exclusively on UK-based, FCA-regulated trading platforms and teach strict risk management from day one. Trading carries significant risk and the majority of retail CFD accounts lose money, which is exactly why disciplined education matters.

What is included in The Trading Desk membership?

The Trading Desk is £97 per month. You get one 90-minute live session each month, full access to the Trading Library, an AI assistant to support your analysis and trade reviews, and training in news and fundamentals, technical analysis and emotional discipline using the EIT™ framework. It suits professionals who want steady structure without daily commitments.

Can a complete beginner learn to trade forex, gold, oil and the Nasdaq?

Yes. Our beginner course, The Market Recruit, assumes no finance background. You will learn price action, market structure, supply and demand, and leave with a written, rules-based trading plan before progressing to live mentorship.
